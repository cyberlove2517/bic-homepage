# PRODUCT.md — BIC Event Experience · Homepage Concept

**Concept anchor:** *Thinking Outside the Box.*
BIC turns ideas into experiences. The homepage dramatizes this literally: a premium box opens, and the brand — plus the idea behind it — comes out.

---

## 1. Homepage objective

- Present BIC as a premium event-experience production house in under 10 seconds.
- Make one idea unforgettable: **the box opens, BIC emerges, the dot becomes the spark.**
- Convert executive interest into an inquiry (primary CTA) and into deeper exploration of proof (secondary CTA → Experience Archive).

This is a concept-level, presentation-ready homepage — not a full site.

## 2. Target impression

The visitor (event procurement lead, marketing director, government protocol office, executive assistant) should feel:

> "This company has taste, discipline, and the confidence to be different. If they treat a homepage like this, they treat an event like this."

Keywords: premium, modern-noir, cinematic, controlled, intelligent — never loud, never generic.

## 3. User journey (narrative arc)

| Beat | Section | Emotion | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Intrigue | Hero — sealed box in a dark scene | Curiosity | Stop the scroll; set the noir tone |
| 2. Reveal | Box opens → BIC emerges → dot escapes as spark | Delight | Deliver the signature "outside the box" moment |
| 3. Statement | Headline + subline + CTAs fade in | Clarity | Say what BIC does, confidently |
| 4. Proof | Trust strip (numbers, regions, clients) | Trust | Quantify credibility |
| 5. Capability | Services grid | Understanding | Show the full production stack |
| 6. Process | From Idea to Experience | Confidence | Show discipline behind the magic |
| 7. Play | Experience Archive (file cabinet) | Engagement | The second signature moment — tactile, memorable, houses video/case links |
| 8. Evidence | Featured work / moments | Desire | Quality over quantity imagery |
| 9. Action | Closing CTA + footer | Commitment | Invite the conversation |

## 4. Section-by-section purpose

1. **Hero** — Signature box-to-BIC GSAP sequence. The box is a conceptual "stage crate": minimal, dark, precisely drawn. It opens; the letters B·I·C lift out; the gray dot breaks free, travels, and ignites as an idea-spark. Headline: *"Thinking outside the box is where we start."* CTAs: *Start a conversation* (primary) / *Open the archive* (secondary, jumps to cabinet).
2. **Trust strip** — 15+ years · 1000+ events · 200+ clients · KSA | Jordan | Kuwait; client name placeholders (Aramco, STC, SABIC, SNB).
3. **Services** — Six capabilities, custom line-icon family: Event Planning & Management; Exhibition Design & Construction; Audio Visual & Technology; Technology Activations; Hospitality & Protocols; Branding & Experience Production.
4. **From Idea to Experience** — Four-step vertical narrative (Listen → Design → Build → Ignite). The dot travels down the timeline and "ignites" the final step — reinforcing the dot-as-spark motif.
5. **Experience Archive (cabinet)** — Noir file-cabinet of numbered drawers. Opening a drawer slides it out and reveals case-file cards: event categories, case-study placeholders, and YouTube video slots. Tactile, keyboard-accessible, mobile-simplified.
6. **Featured work** — Three large cinematic placeholders (Conference / Exhibition / Activation), each carrying the C-arc motif and a case-file number.
7. **Closing CTA** — "Every empty space is a box waiting to be opened." + inquiry CTA.
8. **Footer** — Logo, tagline, regions, contact placeholders, minimal.

## 5. CTA goals

- **Primary:** Start a conversation → `mailto:` placeholder / contact anchor.
- **Secondary:** Open the archive → smooth-scroll to the cabinet (drives interaction with the signature section).

## 6. Content dependencies

- Brand guide PDF (consumed): palette, logo character, typography direction, slogans, stats, client names, website reference page.
- Placeholder copy written in-house (this build) — premium, concise, no buzzwords.
