# icon-prompts.md — BIC Custom Icon Family

**Family name:** *BIC Monoline*
**Style contract (applies to every prompt below):** monochrome line icon, single-weight 2px geometric strokes, architectural precision, straight lines and perfect circular arcs only, no filled shapes except **one small solid dot** placed at the point where the idea "ignites", matte light-gray (#F2F2F2) strokes on a near-black (#1E1E1E) square background, generous negative space, premium Swiss / modern-classical feel, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, centered, 1:1.

Use this preamble verbatim, then append the per-icon line.

---

### 01 — Event Planning & Management
> …a minimal floor-plan grid with a stage block and a timeline ruler along the bottom edge, one solid dot marking the starting point on the timeline.

### 02 — Exhibition Design & Construction
> …an isometric wireframe exhibition booth drawn in thin outline, one corner post rising slightly taller than the others, one solid dot at its top like a beacon.

### 03 — Audio Visual & Technology
> …a speaker cabinet outline with three concentric sound arcs leaving one side and a projector beam cone above it, one solid dot at the arc origin.

### 04 — Technology Activations
> …a stylized hand-free interface: a floating rectangle screen with a circular touch ripple beneath it, one solid dot at the center of the ripple like a trigger point.

### 05 — Hospitality & Protocols
> …a cloche serving dome on a thin base line with a single star-shaped sparkle above it, one solid dot at the tip of the cloche handle.

### 06 — Branding & Experience Production
> …a flag or banner outline on a flagpole with a folded ribbon edge, one solid dot at the top of the pole.
